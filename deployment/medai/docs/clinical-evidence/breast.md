# Breast cancer evidence brief

For oncology-professional review. General evidence navigation only; not patient-specific advice. Recheck the linked source before clinical use.

## Clinical evidence

- NCI Breast Cancer Treatment PDQ (health professional): https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/hp/breast-treatment-pdq
- NCI overview for clinicians, including treatment, screening, research and genetics: https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/hp
- NCI breast biomarker tests (ER, PR, HER2 and related testing): https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis/breast-cancer-biomarker-tests

## Clinical trials

- NCI treatment trials for breast cancer: https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials/disease/breast-cancer/treatment
- NCI guide to finding trials: https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials-search/steps

Use diagnosis/subtype, stage, prior treatment, molecular findings and health status to screen trial criteria; the study team and treating oncologist determine eligibility.

## Supportive care

- Nutrition in Cancer Care (NCI PDQ, health professional): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects/appetite-loss/nutrition-hp-pdq
- Physical activity during and after cancer care (NCI): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/obesity/physical-activity-fact-sheet
- Palliative care in cancer (NCI): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/advanced-cancer/care-choices/palliative-care-fact-sheet

Nutrition and physical activity should be individualized to symptoms, treatment, nutritional status and functional capacity. Palliative care can support symptom management alongside disease-directed treatment; the oncology team should tailor referrals.
