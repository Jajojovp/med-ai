# Non-small cell lung cancer evidence brief

For oncology-professional review. General evidence navigation only; not patient-specific advice. Recheck the linked source before clinical use.

## Clinical evidence

- NCI NSCLC Treatment PDQ (health professional): https://www.cancer.gov/types/lung/hp/non-small-cell-lung-treatment-pdq
- NCI lung cancer overview for clinicians: https://www.cancer.gov/types/lung/hp
- NCI NSCLC treatment trial search: https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials/disease/non-small-cell-lung/treatment

Treatment and trial review depends on histology, stage, tumor location, nodal status, prior treatment, molecular findings, performance status and comorbidities.

## Clinical trials

- NCI guide to finding trials: https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials-search/steps
- ClinicalTrials.gov search: https://clinicaltrials.gov/search?cond=Non-Small%20Cell%20Lung%20Cancer

Search results are leads, not eligibility decisions. The treating oncologist and trial site must confirm current criteria.

## Supportive care

- Nutrition in Cancer Care (NCI PDQ, health professional): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects/appetite-loss/nutrition-hp-pdq
- Physical activity during and after cancer care (NCI): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/obesity/physical-activity-fact-sheet
- Palliative care in cancer (NCI): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/advanced-cancer/care-choices/palliative-care-fact-sheet

Nutrition, activity and symptom management should be individualized to the patient’s respiratory symptoms, treatment effects, nutritional status and functional capacity. Palliative care may be integrated at any stage in coordination with oncology.
