# Gastric cancer evidence brief

For oncology-professional review. General evidence navigation only; not patient-specific advice. Recheck the linked source before clinical use.

## Clinical evidence

- NCI Gastric Cancer Treatment PDQ (health professional): https://www.cancer.gov/types/stomach/hp/stomach-treatment-pdq
- NCI gastric cancer overview for clinicians: https://www.cancer.gov/types/stomach/hp
- NCI information on hereditary gastric cancer: https://www.cancer.gov/types/stomach/hp/gastric-genetics-pdq

Management is based on comprehensive staging, histology, resectability, biomarkers, prior treatment, symptoms and multidisciplinary review.

## Clinical trials

- NCI treatment trials for gastric cancer: https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials/disease/stomach-cancer/treatment
- NCI guide to finding trials: https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials-search/steps
- ClinicalTrials.gov search: https://clinicaltrials.gov/search?cond=Gastric%20Cancer

Trial eligibility needs clinician review of stage, histology, prior treatment, biomarkers, health status and study-specific criteria.

## Supportive care

- Nutrition in Cancer Care (NCI PDQ, health professional): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects/appetite-loss/nutrition-hp-pdq
- Physical activity during and after cancer care (NCI): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/obesity/physical-activity-fact-sheet
- Palliative care in cancer (NCI): https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/advanced-cancer/care-choices/palliative-care-fact-sheet

Nutrition, hydration, activity and symptom support should be individualized to intake, weight change, obstruction or postoperative issues, treatment and functional status. Palliative care can address symptoms and goals alongside active cancer treatment.
